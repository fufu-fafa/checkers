Checkers

Red's turn

How to play
  • Red goes first, then players take turns on this device.
  • Pieces move one square diagonally forward.
  • Jump over a neighboring enemy piece to take it. If you can jump, you must.
  • Keep jumping with the same piece until it has no jumps left.
  • Reach the far row to get a king, which moves forward and backward.
  • You win when your opponent has no pieces left or no legal move.
